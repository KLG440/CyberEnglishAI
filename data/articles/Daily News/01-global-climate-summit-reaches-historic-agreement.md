---
title: "Global Climate Summit Reaches Historic Agreement"
category: Daily News
difficulty: Intermediate
---

World leaders gathered in Geneva this week for the annual Global Climate Summit, where they reached a historic agreement to accelerate the transition to renewable energy. The accord commits participating nations to double their investment in solar and wind power by 2030.

Environmental groups welcomed the agreement but warned that words must be followed by action. "We have seen many promises before," said one activist. "The difference now is that every country has agreed to publish annual progress reports."

The summit also addressed climate adaptation, with a new fund established to help developing nations build flood defenses and drought-resistant agriculture.

Key vocabulary: summit, agreement, transition, renewable, adaptation
