---
title: "City Launches Free Public Wi-Fi Program"
category: Daily News
difficulty: Beginner
---

The city government announced a new program to provide free public Wi-Fi in parks, libraries, and transportation hubs. The initiative aims to bridge the digital divide and give all residents access to online education and government services.

Officials said the network will use advanced security measures to protect users' privacy. "Public Wi-Fi should be safe and accessible for everyone," the mayor stated.

The first phase covers fifty locations, with plans to expand to the entire city within two years. Citizens can connect by simply selecting the network and accepting the terms.

Key vocabulary: initiative, digital, divide, network, privacy
