---
title: "New High-Speed Rail Line Connects Two Major Cities"
category: Daily News
difficulty: Beginner
---

A new high-speed rail line opened this month, cutting travel time between the two cities from five hours to just ninety minutes. The line is expected to carry over 20 million passengers in its first year.

Commuters celebrated the launch. "I can now visit my family every weekend," said one delighted passenger. The rail company says tickets will start at a reasonable price to encourage more people to choose trains over cars.

The project took six years to complete and created thousands of jobs during construction. Local businesses along the route are already reporting increased customers.

Key vocabulary: rail, passengers, commuters, route, construction
