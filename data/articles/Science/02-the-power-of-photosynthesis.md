title: "The Power of Photosynthesis"
category: Science
difficulty: Beginner
---

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into food and oxygen. It is arguably the most important chemical reaction on Earth, producing the oxygen we breathe and forming the base of nearly every food chain.

Inside plant cells, tiny structures called chloroplasts capture light energy. This energy powers a series of reactions that combine carbon dioxide and water into glucose — a sugar that plants use for growth and energy.

Scientists are studying photosynthesis to develop artificial versions that could produce clean fuels or capture carbon from the atmosphere. Understanding this ancient process may hold keys to solving modern energy and climate challenges.

Key vocabulary: photosynthesis, chloroplast, glucose, energy, atmosphere
