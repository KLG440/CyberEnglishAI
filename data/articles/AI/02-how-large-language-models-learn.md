---
title: "How Large Language Models Learn"
category: AI
difficulty: Intermediate
---

Large language models like ChatGPT are trained on enormous amounts of text from books, websites, and articles. During training, the model learns statistical patterns in language — which words tend to follow other words, and in what contexts.

The key technique is called transformer architecture, which allows the model to pay attention to different parts of a sentence simultaneously. This enables the model to understand context and generate coherent responses.

Training these models requires massive computing power. A single large model might use thousands of specialized chips for months. After training, the model can be fine-tuned for specific tasks like translation, summarization, or code generation.

Key vocabulary: language model, training, transformer, architecture, fine-tune
