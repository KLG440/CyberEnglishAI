---
title: "AI in Healthcare: Opportunities and Concerns"
category: AI
difficulty: Upper Intermediate
---

Artificial intelligence is making remarkable progress in healthcare. AI systems can now analyze medical images with accuracy comparable to human radiologists, helping detect diseases like cancer at earlier stages. Predictive algorithms assist doctors in identifying patients at risk of complications.

However, the integration of AI into medicine raises important questions. Who is responsible when an AI makes a diagnostic error? How do we ensure algorithms are fair across different patient populations? And how can we protect sensitive medical data used to train these systems?

Experts argue that AI should augment, not replace, human clinicians. The most successful implementations combine machine analysis with human judgment, allowing doctors to focus on patient care while AI handles data-intensive tasks.

Key vocabulary: artificial intelligence, diagnostic, algorithm, augment, clinician
