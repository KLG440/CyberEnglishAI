---
title: "Ransomware: Understanding the Modern Digital Extortion"
category: Cybersecurity
difficulty: Intermediate
---

Ransomware is a type of malicious software that encrypts a victim's files and demands payment to restore access. It has become one of the most profitable and dangerous forms of cybercrime.

Modern ransomware attacks often follow a double extortion model: attackers not only encrypt data but also threaten to leak it publicly if the ransom is not paid. This puts enormous pressure on organizations, especially those handling sensitive customer information.

Defense against ransomware requires a layered approach: regular offline backups, up-to-date patching, email filtering, and employee training. Most importantly, organizations should have an incident response plan ready before an attack occurs, because recovery decisions are difficult to make under pressure.

Key vocabulary: ransomware, encryption, extortion, backup, incident response
